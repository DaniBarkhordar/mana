# Mananu brand

**Positioning line:** Weigh it. Don't guess it.

## Why it looks like this

The category is saturated with mint-green and lilac wellness apps. Mananu is
warm graphite and brass — closer to a set of kitchen scales or a measuring tool
than to a meditation app. The mark is three stacked strokes of equal mass — the three syllables of the
name, and a balance at rest. The middle stroke is brass: the measured one.

## Colours

| **Estimated** | `#9A7B4F` | **Reserved: values that were guessed** |
| Warning | `#B4531E` | Over target, off-protocol readings |
| Danger | `#B03A2E` | Destructive actions |
| Protein / Carbs / Fat | `#2F6F8F` / `#C8912F` / `#8A5A9B` | Macro series — differ in lightness as well as hue |

The measured/estimated pair is not decoration. It carries the product's core
claim, so it is used for nothing else.

## Type

A plain grotesque (Inter, or the platform default). Tabular figures anywhere a
number can change — without them a live weight climbing from 118 g to 119 g
visibly shifts sideways, which on a scale readout looks broken.

mananu sets lowercase with tight tracking. Three open syllables, ma-na-nu, no
consonant clusters: anyone who hears it once spells it right. The three-stroke
mark carries the app icon and the scale silkscreen.
