How these numbers are worked out
Your scale passes a small current through your body and measures the resistance. Water conducts; fat does not. Everything except your weight is calculated from that one measurement using published equations — Sun (2003) for fat-free mass and body water, Janssen (2000) for skeletal muscle, Cunningham (1980) for resting energy. Bioimpedance is good at showing change over weeks and poor at absolute figures for one person on one day. Mananu shows you the trend for that reason.
