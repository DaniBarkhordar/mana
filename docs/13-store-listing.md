## Description

Photo calorie apps guess how much is on your plate, and portion size is where they go wrong. Mananu uses the camera only to work out what the food is. The amount comes off the scale.

**Weigh, don't estimate.** Put the bowl on your Mananu kitchen scale and add one ingredient at a time. Mananu captures each one from the change in weight, so you never empty the bowl and never type a number.

**Name it with a photo.** Take a photo and Mananu names what is on the plate, matched to the UK food database. It never guesses grams; that is the scale's job.

**The fat nobody counts.** Weigh the pan, add the oil, weigh what is left. The difference went into your food, and it is the one thing a photo can never see.

**Body composition you can trust the direction of.** Your Mananu body scale measures weight and impedance. Body fat, muscle and water are calculated on your phone with published, cited equations, shown with their error, as a 7-day trend rather than a single number to obsess over.

**Your wearable on the same timeline.** Connect Apple Health or Health Connect and last night's sleep, HRV and resting heart rate sit next to this morning's reading, each labelled with the device that recorded it.

**Recipes.** Weigh the ingredients once, weigh the finished dish, then log a portion by weight forever.

**Free forever:** weighing, barcode scanning, manual logging, targets, body trends and Health sync.
**Mananu Plus** (£4.99 a month or £39.99 a year): photo recognition without limit beyond thirty scans a month, recipes, and personal calibration.

**Private by design.** Everything is stored on your phone first and backed up to your account in the UK. No ads, no analytics services, no selling data. Export everything or delete everything from inside the app.

Mananu is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Do not use the body scale if you have a pacemaker or another implanted electronic device.
